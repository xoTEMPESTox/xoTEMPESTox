## About Me

At Liferythm Healthcare, I work on an AI platform spanning patient intake, clinical documentation, triage, follow-up, and AI reception, with shared agentic workflows and Bluetooth medical-device integrations. 

Previously, I built realtime speech-to-speech AI at Creo AI , RAG systems with over 500+ hours of trading knowledge at Chart Raiders, and enterprise Graph-RAG/tool-calling systems.  

Outside work, I maintain [**TradingViewPlus**](https://github.com/xoTEMPESTox/TradingviewPlus), an open-source TypeScript extension used by 300+ traders, contribute to open source, and keep up with emerging AI research, including world models.
